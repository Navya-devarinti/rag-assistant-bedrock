# Bluepeak Analytics — Data Platform FAQ

*This is a fictional sample document written for the RAG assistant demo.*

## Where do I find the dashboards?

All team dashboards live in the Bluepeak Metrics Portal at
metrics.bluepeak.example.com. Sign in with your company email and MFA.
If you cannot see a dashboard you need, ask the dashboard owner to add you.

## How do I request access to a dataset?

File an access request in the Data Hub and name the dataset, the business
reason, and how long you need access. Most requests are approved within
2 business days. Access to customer personal data needs an extra privacy
review, which can take up to 5 business days.

## How long is raw event data kept?

Raw event logs are kept for 90 days. Aggregated tables are kept for 2 years.
If you need data older than that, open a ticket with the data platform team
and they will check the cold-storage archive.

## Who do I contact for support?

The data platform team holds office hours every Wednesday from 2:00 to
3:00 p.m. Eastern in the #data-platform Slack channel. For urgent pipeline
outages, page the on-call engineer through the support runbook.
