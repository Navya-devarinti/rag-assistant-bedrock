# Bluepeak Analytics — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

*This is a fictional sample document written for the RAG assistant demo.
Bluepeak Analytics is an invented company; none of this is real policy.*

## Remote Work

Bluepeak Analytics is a remote-first company. Employees may work from any
location in the United States. Teams hold a weekly video standup on Mondays
at 10:00 a.m. Eastern, and cameras are optional. Employees who prefer an
office may book a desk at any of our coworking partner locations.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar
year, plus 10 company holidays. PTO requests of more than 3 consecutive days
should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance. Unused PTO does not carry
over to the next year, except where local law requires it.

## Security

All employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on every
company account. Production systems must never be accessed from public
Wi-Fi networks. If a laptop is lost or stolen, report it to the IT team
within 24 hours so the device can be locked remotely.

## Expenses

Work-related expenses up to $100 per month do not need pre-approval. Larger
purchases, such as monitors or conference travel, need a manager's written
approval before you spend. Submit receipts within 30 days of the purchase.
