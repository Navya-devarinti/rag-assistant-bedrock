Maple & Main — Returns Policy
================================

This is a fictional sample document written for the RAG assistant demo.
Maple & Main is an invented online store.

You may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

Return shipping is free. Print a return label from your order page and drop
the package at any post office. Refunds are issued to your original payment
method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the return.

The following items are final sale and cannot be returned: gift cards,
personalized items, and clearance items marked "final sale" at checkout.

If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact us within 14 days and we
will send a replacement or a refund, including return shipping, at no cost
to you.
